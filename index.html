About

Researcher with a practical focus.

I am a researcher and cloud/AI platform engineer with hands-on enterprise experience. I work on agentic AI, distributed systems, and cloud reliability, with a focus on what it takes to use these systems safely and reliably in production.

I am pursuing a Doctorate in Computer Science at the University of the Potomac. My research is shaped by real delivery work, so I care about more than an idea working in theory.it also needs to be secure, observable, governed, and useful to the people who run it.
