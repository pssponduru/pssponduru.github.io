About

Research grounded in the realities of production systems.

I work at the intersection of AI, cloud infrastructure, and operational reliability. My research focuses on keeping advanced systems useful while making their decisions safer, observable, governed, and reversible.

I am pursuing a Doctorate in Computer Science at the University of the Potomac and bring hands-on experience building cloud and AI platforms across complex enterprise environments.
